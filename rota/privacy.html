Rota

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Rota helps you keep track of recurring tasks and who did them. That's your household's business, so the app is built so that we never see any of it.

We collect nothing

Rota has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, task data, usage data or diagnostics. The app makes no network requests.

What Rota stores

Your tasks (name, schedule, time, note), the people you add (name, emoji, color), which of them checked off what and when, and your settings. All of it is stored only on your device, and included in your device's own backup (iCloud or computer) if you use one, under Apple's terms.

Partners

Partners are names you type in. They don't sign in, and nothing is sent to them. Please only add people who are happy to be included.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. When you tap Done on a notification, the app records it on your device. Background App Refresh is used only to schedule upcoming reminders.

Sharing and exports

PDF reports, CSV exports and Rota backups are created on your device and sent only where you choose, using the share sheet.

Children

Rota is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting data

Delete a task or a partner in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
