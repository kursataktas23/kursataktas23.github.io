Help & Support

Reminders don't arrive

Check Settings › Notifications › Rota and make sure notifications are allowed. Check that the task has "Remind me" turned on with at least one reminder, and that it isn't paused. Focus modes can hold notifications back. A reminder doesn't ring if everyone on the task has already done it.

Marking a task done from the notification

Press and hold the notification (or swipe it left and tap View) and tap Done. For a shared task you can choose "Done by everyone" or "Done by me".

Partners

Add partners in Settings › Partners. They don't need the app. Choose who does each task in the task's settings. On the Today list, the round button checks a task off for everyone on it; tap a person's picture to check it off just for them. Choose "New tasks are shared with" to share new tasks automatically.

Several times a day, set days, any day

A task can have several times a day; each is its own check and its own reminder. Without times, set how many times (e.g. 8 glasses of water) and tap the counter each time. Weekly and monthly tasks can fall on set days or dates, on a weekday like "the first Monday", or any day of the week or month.

Weekly and monthly tasks

A weekly task can be done on any day of its week and is due by the day you chose. If the week ends without it, it counts as not done. Monthly tasks work the same way within the month. "Skip this time" doesn't count against you.

I forgot to check something off

Open the task, tap the day (or week) in the calendar or the history, and set who did it and when.

Moving to a new phone

Settings › Back up everything creates one small file. Save it to Files, then on the new phone tap Restore from a backup. Your device's iCloud backup also includes Rota.
