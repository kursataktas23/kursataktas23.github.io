Rota

Every day, every week, every month. Together.

Rota reminds you of the things that keep coming back, lets you check them off right from the notification, and keeps track of who did what. For you, your partner, your roommates or your family. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Any schedule: several times a day (medicine at 8, 14 and 20), set weekdays, a few times a week, the 5th and the 7th of the month, the first Monday, every 2 weeks.
  • Up to five reminders per task: at the time, the evening before or days ahead. Press and hold the notification and tap Done.
  • Add partners. One check marks a shared task done for everyone on it, or tap a face to mark it for just one person.
  • "Done by…": who did it and when. Fix the history if you forgot to check something.
  • Reports: done and not done by week, month and year, by person and by task, with on-time rates and streaks.
  • PDF report and CSV export. Back up everything to one small file.
  • In English, Turkish, German, Spanish and French.

Private by design

Your tasks, partners and history stay on your device. No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Partners don't need the app or an account.

Free

Rota is free. No ads, no subscription, no limits on tasks or partners.
