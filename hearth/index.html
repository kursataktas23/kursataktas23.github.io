Hearth

Know what you own, before you need to prove it.

Hearth turns your home into an insurance-ready inventory. Snap a photo, and your iPhone recognizes the item, reads serial numbers and receipts, and keeps everything organised by room. One tap exports a PDF your insurer understands.

Renting out a room or a home? Create a move-in or move-out handover report that lists every item with its photo and condition, with signature lines for landlord and tenant.

Private by design. No account, no servers, no tracking. All recognition happens on your device and your inventory never leaves it.
