Hearth

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Hearth is built so that we never see your data. This policy explains what that means in practice.

We collect nothing

Hearth has no account system, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, usage data or diagnostics.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter (items, photos, receipts, values and notes) is stored only on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, your data is also stored in your own private iCloud database, which is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.

Your data is included in your normal device backups (iCloud Backup or computer backups) as controlled by you.

Photos and camera

Hearth asks for camera access only so you can photograph belongings and receipts. Photos you pick from your library are copied into the app. Recognition of objects, text, serial numbers and receipt totals runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision and Apple Intelligence frameworks. No image or text is uploaded anywhere.

Face ID

If you enable App Lock, authentication is handled by iOS. Hearth never receives your biometric data.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Hearth checks your purchase status on the device using StoreKit.

Exports

When you export a PDF or CSV report, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Children

Hearth does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. If you use iCloud Sync, you can also remove Hearth's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
