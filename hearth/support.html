Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone only. With iCloud Sync on (Pro), it's also in your private iCloud account. We never see it.

How do I move to a new phone?
Restore your iPhone from a backup, or turn on iCloud Sync before switching. As an extra safety net, export a PDF and CSV and keep them in Files or email them to yourself.

Why doesn't the app fill in brand or model?
Brand, model and serial number are only filled in when they are visible as text in the photo. On devices with Apple Intelligence, suggestions are more detailed. Everything can be edited.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings in Hearth and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings › Hearth Pro › Manage Subscription, or in the iOS Settings app under your name › Subscriptions. The lifetime purchase never renews.

Is Hearth's report accepted by insurers?
The report is a clear, itemised record with photos, values and receipts, which is what insurers typically ask for. Acceptance is always up to your insurer.
